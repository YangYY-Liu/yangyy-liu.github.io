I am a Ph.D. student at the [University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC)](https://en.uestc.edu.cn/), supervised by Prof. Shi Gu at the Brain and Intelligence Lab. I received my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology (Big Data) from [Xidian University](https://en.xidian.edu.cn/) in 2020.

My research interests include multimodal learning, medical imaging, explainable AI, concept bottleneck models, vision-language models, and image registration. I was a research intern at the Medical AI Lab of [Alibaba DAMO Academy](https://damo.alibaba.com/) and at Inclusion AI, Ant Group.
